# Ready-made sounds for the live studio

Everything here was stated as CC0 (public domain) on its own source page when it was collected on 2026-11-06, so no
attribution is required and commercial use is allowed. Credit is given anyway. Files were levelled and, for effects,
shortened, with ffmpeg. `sounds.json` is what the studio reads.

"Bossa Shop Theme" is offered by its author under CC0 or CC-BY 3.0; it is used here under CC0.

## The sound of a piece arriving (reveal/)

Shimmering chime, Whoosh and chime, Harp sweep, Shop door bell and Pop and sparkle were made for this shop in code,
with no outside recording in them: `.ftp-sync/tools/make-reveal-sounds.py`. Sparkle and Camera flashes are the
start of `fx/sparkle.mp3` and `fx/camera-flash.mp3` above, shortened and levelled.
